## The problem PARDES solves

<CardGrid stagger>
	<Card title="Inconsistency kills trust" icon="warning">
		You've been using Claude, Cursor, or Copilot for months. One day you ship in two hours
		what would have taken a week. The next day you burn the whole afternoon fixing an *output*
		that looked right. The cause is almost never the model — it's the intent that wasn't pinned
		down before the *prompt*.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Intent first" icon="star">
		The only exclusively human act in software development is saying *what this exists for*.
		Everything else — design, contracts, code, tests, *deploy* — can be derived by agents,
		provided that intent has been captured faithfully.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Fifteen linked steps" icon="list-format">
		From Intent to Evolution, fifteen steps alternating *spec* and execution. Not *waterfall*,
		not bureaucratic documentation, not ceremony. It's the chain that makes AI deliver with
		predictability instead of luck.
	</Card>
	<Card title="In 2 minutes with pardes init" icon="rocket">
		You don't have to learn the whole method before starting. You run `pardes init` on one of
		your own *features*, pick your AI agent — Claude Code, Cursor, Windsurf, Copilot, Gemini,
		Aider — paste with Cmd+V, and the agent takes over the deep interview with you. You walk
		out feeling the method in your gut. [Get to know the CLI →](/en/cli/)
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
