PARDES — the method, the CLI, the site, and everything that orbits around them — is developed in [Leonardo Mendonça](/en/sobre-o-autor/)'s spare time, by a software architect, and released under an open license so any dev can use it without friction.

If the method helps you ship with more predictability in your work — if it saves you the hours you used to spend fixing AI outputs, if it gives you a shared vocabulary with your team, if it simply lifted the weight of thinking how to phrase things — you can give back.

## GitHub Sponsors

The most direct way is through **[GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/LeonardoMendoncaDev)**. You support from a few dollars a month, cancel any time, and you help fund the time dedicated to:

- keeping the CLI working on the main operating systems;
- writing new content on the site (more examples, more cases, more translations);
- answering issues and honest questions that arrive by email or GitHub;
- exploring the next steps of the method (steps 3 to 15 of the chain, auxiliary tools, integrations with other agents).

## Other ways to support

Supporting isn't only money. These count for even more:

- **Use the method** on a feature of yours and see what changes — that's the most honest signal you can send.
- **Share a real case** — if you tried it and it went well (or badly), open an issue on the [site repository](https://github.com/LeonardoMendoncaDev/pardes) with a short report. Cases teach more than slogans.
- **Point out what's missing** — if a page is confusing, if an analogy fails, if a manifesto sentence sounds pretentious, say so. Honest criticism is worth gold.
- **Talk about PARDES** with colleagues who complain about AI inconsistency. Word of mouth from someone living the problem is the shortest path for the method to reach who needs it.

Thanks for reading this far. That's already more than the average.
